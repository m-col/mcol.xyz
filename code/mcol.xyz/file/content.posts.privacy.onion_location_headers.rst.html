      1 tor browser can now redirect clearnet sites to onion services
      2 =============================================================
      3 
      4 :date: 2020-06-03
      5 :summary: tor browser has a new feature that redirects clearnet sites to their
      6           onion services
      7 
      8 On Monday the Tor Project released_ version 9.5 of the tor browser and with it
      9 a long-awaited new feature. The update will tell you if a website you visit in
     10 the browser has a corresponding onion service.
     11 
     12 Initially, when you visit one of these websites (such as this one), the URL bar
     13 will display this label:
     14 
     15 .. image:: /static/onion_location.png
     16    :alt: Tor Browser displaying '.onion available' in URL bar.
     17 
     18 Clicking on this label will take you to the onion service version of the
     19 website.
     20 
     21 And the cool part: you can set tor browser to always redirect you to these
     22 onion services, by finding this preference and setting it to 'Always':
     23 
     24 .. image:: /static/onion_location_pref.png
     25    :alt: Tor Browser onion-location pref
     26 
     27 This functionality depends on websites setting a :code:`Onion-Location` HTTP
     28 header containing the link to their onion service. For example, all I had to do
     29 was add this line into my nginx.conf in my clearnet site's server block:
     30 
     31 .. code-block:: sh
     32 
     33     add_header Onion-Location http://mcolxyzogp3cy4czf52oa2svu2vjge3otm3shxmtvwshyum47sis3iid.onion$request_uri;
     34 
     35 That header is all it takes for tor browser users to use onions by default on
     36 all sites that host them, removing any need for manual identification of
     37 whether websites that they visit host onion services.
     38 
     39 By improving awareness and accessibility of onion services in this way, not
     40 only can we discover more onions for popular websites that may have them, but
     41 we legitimise and encourage the use of onion services as a simple means of
     42 making the web more accessible for those who experience internet censorship.
     43 
     44 
     45 .. _released : https://blog.torproject.org/new-release-tor-browser-95
     46
