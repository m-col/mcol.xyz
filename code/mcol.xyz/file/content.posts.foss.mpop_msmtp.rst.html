      1 mpop and msmtp: a minimalist match made in heaven
      2 =================================================
      3 
      4 :date: 2019-05-28
      5 :summary: mpop and msmtp: a minimalist match made in heaven
      6 
      7 Like many sane people, I have a soft spot for little C programs with little
      8 config files that do exactly what they do and nothing else. `Martin Lambers'
      9 <https://marlam.de/>`_ POP3 and SMTP clients, `mpop <https://marlam.de/mpop/>`_
     10 and `msmtp <https://marlam.de/msmtp/>`_, are perfect examples.
     11 
     12 In my quest for a minimal and straight forward setup, these bad boys have
     13 become my daily email handlers, with :code:`mpop` downloading my emails into a
     14 Maildir at :code:`~/.mail`, :code:`mutt` being a mostly offline interface to
     15 deal with all the crap people send me, and :code:`msmtp` as the backend to send
     16 mail from mutt.
     17 
     18 mpop can be run as a cronjob, downloading mail every 30 minutes, leaving no
     19 trace on the remote server (if I trust my provider, that is).
     20 
     21 setup
     22 -----
     23 
     24 mpop and msmtp can be installed from your distributions repos via the usual
     25 route.
     26 
     27 Both programs have sane defaults that would work well for a standard user, so
     28 the configuration files are very few lines and take only a minute to write.
     29 
     30 On top of that, they both share a chunk of code and its corresponding options,
     31 resulting in a good bit of the two files sharing lines.
     32 
     33 My config for mpop, for example, is simply:
     34 
     35 .. code-block:: haskell
     36 
     37     defaults
     38     uidls_file "~/.config/mpop/uidls_%U_at_%H"
     39     tls on
     40     tls_starttls off
     41     
     42     account posteo
     43     host "posteo.de"
     44     user "<myemailaddress>"
     45     passwordeval "gpg --no-tty -q -d --for-your-eyes-only ~/.mail/posteo.gpg"
     46     delivery maildir "~/.mail/posteo/INBOX"
     47     port 995
     48 
     49 Compared to my nearly identical msmtp config:
     50 
     51 .. code-block:: haskell
     52 
     53     defaults
     54     auth on
     55     tls on
     56     tls_starttls off
     57 
     58     account posteo
     59     host "posteo.de"
     60     user "<myemailaddress>"
     61     from "<myemailaddress>"
     62     passwordeval "gpg --no-tty -q -d --for-your-eyes-only ~/.mail/posteo.gpg"
     63     port 465
     64 
     65 The first 4 lines of each are the defaults, which apply to all email accounts
     66 that are set up. The second block is therefore my first account, in this case
     67 just named :code:`posteo` for my provider. Everything is simple and self
     68 explanatory.  Of note though is the passwordeval lines, which is simply a shell
     69 line executed that should give you the password for that account, in this case
     70 taking it from my gpg-agent.
     71 
     72 torifying
     73 ---------
     74 
     75 Both programs can be run straight through Tor with no trouble at all, just
     76 stick this into the defaults sections:
     77 
     78 .. code-block:: haskell
     79 
     80     proxy_host localhost
     81     proxy_port 9050
     82 
     83 After 2 minutes of setup, these programs can work in the background and never
     84 ask for your attention again.
