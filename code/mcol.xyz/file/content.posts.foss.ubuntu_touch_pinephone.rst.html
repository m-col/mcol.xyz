      1 ubuntu-touch on the pinephone: first impressions
      2 ================================================
      3 
      4 :date: 2020-02-11
      5 :summary: My first impression of testing ubuntu-touch on the pinephone
      6           BraveHeart release
      7 
      8 My Braveheart edition PinePhone_ arrived a couple of days after a *long*
      9 journey and I've finally got some time to play with it. The first thing I
     10 wanted to do was test drive ubuntu-touch_, the mobile version of Ubuntu
     11 maintained by the `UBports community`_, and I figured I'd write about my
     12 experiences.
     13 
     14 
     15 interface and responsiveness
     16 ----------------------------
     17 
     18 My expectations were sensibly quite low given that it's still early days for
     19 the pinephone, but I've got to say, I was pleasantly surprised by how
     20 responsive and un-frustrating the UI is at this stage.
     21 
     22 The keyboard, which sometimes was an issue for me when I tested postmarketOS_,
     23 works 99% perfectly. There does appear to be a minor bug where keyboard sounds,
     24 when enabled in the settings, are only triggered on the first key press after
     25 the keyboard is opened. A low priority bug to be sure.
     26 
     27 The app draw - displayed when swiping in from the left of the screen - feels
     28 intuitive and looks great. Much more convenient access to apps than android
     29 has. Swiping in the from the right of the screen gives a view of all running
     30 apps, allow you to swipe them up to close them. These two very gesture-based
     31 features are smooth and responsive and don't feel clunky at all.
     32 
     33 .. image:: /static/UT_pinephone.jpg
     34     :width: 60%
     35     :align: center
     36     :alt: ubuntu-touch on pinephone: view of running apps
     37 
     38 Dragging the bar at the top of the screen down (although *not* swiping down
     39 from off the top of the screen) dispays a number of informational tabs much
     40 like in android and iOS. There has been a little :fas:`envelope` icon on the
     41 bar since it first booted suggesting a notifications pending, but the
     42 notifications tab is pretty convinced that that is not the case.

     48 
     49 hardware
     50 --------
     51 
     52 Much of the hardware is perfectly under control:
     53 
     54  - The volume buttons do indeed change the volume.
     55  - The lock/power button locks the screen, and turns it back on again.
     56  - Screen brightness control works.
     57 
     58 But some is not:
     59 
     60  - Wifi appears to connect and work beautifully initially, but then fails.
     61    There is a working fix for when it does, however (discussion on `ubports
     62    <https://forums.ubports.com/topic/3791/wifi-issues-on-pinephone-braveheart/8>`_
     63    and `pine64 <https://forum.pine64.org/showthread.php?tid=8969>`_).
     64  - The *Rotation Lock* setting appears to do nothing; perhaps the gyro doesn't
     65    quite work yet.
     66  - Currently pinephone ubuntu-touch is stuck in headphone mode, and has other
     67    issues with the speakers (`pine64 discussion
     68    <https://forum.pine64.org/showthread.php?tid=8923>`_).
     69  - There also appears to be a an issue with the battery usage, that `others
     70    have also experienced`_, though this does appear to be an issue that can be
     71    fixed by software changes (fortunately!).
     72 
     73 
     74 software
     75 --------
     76 
     77 It does seem pretty strange, SSH-ing into and exploring a phone from my
     78 computer. Using apt to install tor or vim or whatever the hell I want. On my
     79 phone. That novelty is yet to wear off.
     80 
     81 A dozen or so apps came pre-installed with ubuntu-touch, and more can be
     82 downloaded from the *OpenStore*. Many of these are simple web-apps, which has
     83 enabled a huge range of 'apps' to be created already. Though some are buggy
     84 (unsurprising, considering the webpages they display probably weren't designed
     85 with this in mind), they do serve as stepping stones between having no apps and
     86 eventually having native apps :fas:`praying-hands`.
     87 
     88 We are in the midst of an incredibly exciting time for linux smart phones, and
     89 I can't wait to see what the next few months bring us. I'm sure very soon we
     90 will have all the necessary components of the pinephone working smoothly, and
     91 once people make that transition to using it as a(n imperfect, yet functional)
     92 daily driver, the new apps and functionality will hopefully boom.
