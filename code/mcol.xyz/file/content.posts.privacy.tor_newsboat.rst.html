      1 read news and reddit anonymously with tor and newsboat
      2 ======================================================
      3 
      4 :date: 2019-04-21
      5 :summary: read news and reddit anonymously with tor and newsboat
      6 
      7 ncurses based programs can be a bit hit-or-miss but newsboat is one of the
      8 kings.
      9 
     10 `newsboat <https://newsboat.org/>`_ is an RSS reader, and as far as I'm
     11 concerned, a reddit interface, as you can turn any reddit feed into an RSS
     12 feed. Just stick something like this into your :code:`~/.config/newsboat/urls`
     13 file:
     14 
     15 .. code-block:: conf
     16 
     17     https://www.reddit.com/r/onions/new/.rss?sort=new  "~/r/onions"
     18 
     19 Each time your run newsboat you'll get posts to that subreddit sorted by new -
     20 easy subscription without going near a web browser.
     21 
     22 The key to torifying newsboat is its socks5 proxy, which is enabled with these
     23 config settings in :code:`~/.config/newsboat/config`:
     24 
     25 .. code-block:: conf
     26 
     27     use-proxy yes
     28     proxy-type socks5h
     29     proxy 127.0.0.1:9050
     30 
     31 The proxy IP and port of course must correspond to the socks5 proxy set up in
     32 your torrc config. The **h** in :code:`socks5h` is important - this makes tor
     33 do all its own DNS resolutions so they aren't leaking out the usual route.
     34 
     35 You can take the functionality to the next level by opening any feed items in
     36 tor-browser directly from newsboat, too. First, stick this into your newsboat
     37 config:
     38 
     39 .. code-block:: bash
     40 
     41     browser "nohup tor-browser --allow-remote %u > /dev/null 2>&1 &"
     42 
     43 and set the setting :code:`browser.tabs.loadDivertedInBackground` in
     44 tor-browser's :code:`about:config` to false. This allows feed items to be
     45 opened from newsboat when hitting :code:`o` without tor-browser stealing focus.
     46 
     47 nohup is needed so tor-browser can stay alive even after your close newsboat,
     48 if newsboat opened it. :code:`--allow-remote` will launch tor-browser and allow
     49 it to receive remote commands to open urls as we are doing here. If
     50 tor-browser, with this option, is already running then the url will be opened
     51 in that instance but newsboat will stay focussed. The rest of the line sends
     52 all output to oblivion and backgrounds the command so that it doesn't disrupt
     53 your newsboating.
     54 
     55 With this setup I just open newsboat once a day and give it a couple minutes to
     56 update its feeds while I do something else. Then I can quickly comb through
     57 everything, opening anything I want to read more about in tor-browser as I go
     58 and deleting the rest.
