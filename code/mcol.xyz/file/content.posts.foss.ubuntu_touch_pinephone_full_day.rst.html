      5 :summary: a full day test-driving ubuntu touch on the pinephone
      6 
      7 
      8 My desk has had the Braveheart Edition of the `Pinephone
      9 <https://wiki.pine64.org/index.php/PinePhone>`_ progressively gathering dust,
     10 only seeing attention with a monthly flash of `Ubuntu Touch
     11 <https://ubuntu-touch.io>`_ and a quick play around to check out the current
     12 state. This week I reached the tipping point where UT finally had decent
     13 performance and I was feeling particularly annoyed at my crappy android LG
     14 phone, so yesterday I installed all the apps I might need with the intent of
     15 spending my Sunday using only the pinephone. This is my experience from that
     16 day.
     17 
     18 
     19 11:00
     20 -----
     21 
     22 *100% charge*
     23 
     24 Bluetooth won't turn off so hopefully that won't be too much of a power drain.
     25 I need to leave wifi on - as i do with my "current" phone - but I only ever
     26 turn 4G on when I need it (which is not often). Camera isn't working on UT yet.
     27 
     28 Sending and receiving Signal messages works great with `Axolotl
     29 <https://axolotl.chat/>`_, but it doesn't seem to read contact information from
     30 the Contacts app, so at the minute I'm just seeing phone numbers and hoping I
     31 don't message the wrong person. It also doesn't support push notifications or
     32 phone calls :fas:`sad-tear`.
     33 
     34 
     35 12:00
     36 -----
     37 
     38 *94% charge*
     39 
     40 I have spent maybe 10 minutes with the screen on doing some light messaging
     41 with Axolotl, sent a few texts, and checked Slack :fas:`grimace` using a web
     42 app created by `Martin Kozub <https://zubozrout.cz>`_. If the battery indicator
     43 is reliable then it should be able to last the day unless I start blasting
     44 `tunes <https://asiwyfa.bandcamp.com/album/heirs-2>`_.

     60 This is probably the first time I've taken my pinephone outside, and I am at

    102 Then I sat browsing reddit for 30 minutes or so using `Quickddit
    103 <https://github.com/accumulator/Quickddit>`_, finally getting a 'Low Battery'
    104 notification at 7pm with 9% battery. This prompted me to stop wasting my time
    105 on reddit and put the phone down. When I checked it again 10 minutes later it

    108 
    109 Closing Thoughts
    110 ----------------
    111 
    112 All in all, I got a decent bit of use out it, and I definitely used it more
    113 than I would normally use my phone. In total it lasted around 8 hours.
    114 Considering what it has been like in the past, this is a pretty big improvement
    115 and definitely makes me more excited for how things can be. Of course, some
    116 pinephone-specific things need to be worked out, such as the camera and GPS -
    117 though these may be specific to Ubuntu Touch as I think others working on other
    118 OSes have got the camera working.
    119 
    120 The app ecosystem for UT has also grown considerably in the past few months,
    121 and I'm amazed that I can send and receive Signal messages. I couldn't get any
    122 of the WhatsApp clients from the Open Store to work but clearly that must be a
    123 sign.
    124 
    125 Although there are a number of big annoyances, UT on the pinephone has (for me)
    126 reached the point where I would happily use it over android, so I think for the
    127 time being I will keep trying to use it daily. I will, however, shift a bit of
    128 focus onto `Mobian <https://mobian-project.org>`_ and maybe give that a whirl.
